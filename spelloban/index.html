How to play

  • 1Tap a cell to walk there (shortest path).
  • 2Swipe toward a crate to push it. Arrows / WASD work too.
  • 3Push every crate onto its dashed slot to spell the hidden word.
  • Assist blasts one inner wall so you can finish a hard puzzle. It costs you a star.

Fewer moves earn more stars. A fresh puzzle daily — gentle on Monday, toughest by the weekend.
